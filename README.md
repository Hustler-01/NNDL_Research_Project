# Alzheimer's Detection using Deep Learning
### Abstract
Alzheimer's disease (AD) is a chronic progressive neurodegenerative illness with symptoms in memory, cognition, and behavior. Early detection is crucial to manage symptoms and halt disease progression. In this project, we examine the application of deep learning models to detect Alzheimer's based on MRI scans from the OASIS dataset. We develop and compare VGG16, VGG19, AlexNet, ConvNeXT, ResNet50, EfficientNetB0, InceptionV3 and MobileNetV2 to identify MRI scans as per various stages of Alzheimer's. The models are trained and tested on preprocessed brain MRI scans with comparison of performance metrics in terms of accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score.  Our best-performing model i.e., AlexNet achieved an accuracy of 100%, demonstrating the potential of deep learning techniques in aiding the early diagnosis of Alzheimer's Disease. This work makes an original contribution towards AI-aided early neurodegenerative disease diagnosis.
